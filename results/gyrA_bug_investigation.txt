gyrA feature construction bug — investigation and fix

Root cause: gene_gyrA.csv pools 5 evidence types (BLAT, K-mer Search,
AMRFinderPlus:POINTP, RGI protein variant model, BLASTP). Only POINTP
represents an actual point-mutation call; the others are homology/
presence detectors that fire on the wild-type gene itself.

BUGGY feature (matched on Antibiotics Class only, all evidence types):
  UK susceptible carriage:     39.0%
  Norway susceptible carriage: 100.0%

STRICT feature (POINTP evidence only, quinolone class only):
  UK susceptible carriage:     8.9%
  Norway susceptible carriage: 4.9%
  UK resistant carriage:       99.0% (unchanged)
  Norway resistant carriage:   95.2%

Conclusion: the apparent 'Norway anomaly' was an artifact of pooling
non-mutation evidence types into a mutation-presence feature. Real
discriminative signal survives after correction; false-positive noise
in susceptible genomes does not.
