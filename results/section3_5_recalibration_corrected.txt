Section 3.5 recalibration experiment, corrected features

Before recalibration: Norway intercept -0.488, South Asia intercept +0.927
(both substantially smaller in magnitude than original paper's -1.156/+1.625,
consistent with the gyrA/qnrB bug fixes)

Recalibration works at small N in both populations, replicating the original
finding qualitatively. HOWEVER: mean-based summary at South Asia N=200 is
numerically unreliable -- 17/300 reps (5.7%) show extreme intercepts
(as low as -38.76) from near-perfect separation in unregularized logistic
regression on a 38-genome held-out set (238 total - 200 recal = 38 held out).
Median intercept at N=200 is 0.249 (sane, consistent with trend), but mean
is -2.122 (distorted). Reporting median/IQR instead of mean/percentile for
this reason. This numerical instability was not identified or reported in
the original paper.
