Section 3.4 mechanism analysis, corrected features

Mechanism composition among true resistant genomes:
                        parC_mutation   acquired_only   neither
UK (n=730):             98.5%           0.1%            1.4%
Norway (n=311):         88.1%           1.3%            10.6%
South Asia (n=159):     94.3%           4.4%            1.3%

parC_mutation and acquired_gene_no_parC percentages closely replicate
the original (pre-correction) paper -- these categories don't depend
on the gyrA feature, so the gyrA bug never affected them.

NEW FINDING (not present in original paper): the 'neither' category is
substantially larger and Norway-specific. 33 Norway resistant genomes
(10.6%) carry none of the 8 engineered features at all. Model mean
predicted P(resistant) for these genomes = 0.042, vs 0.978 for
parC-mutation resistant genomes in the same population -- the model is
essentially blind to whatever mechanism explains their resistance.
These genomes span 17 distinct MLST types (largest single type, ST131,
is only 24% of the group), ruling out a single clonal artifact.
UK's equivalent group is much smaller (10 genomes, 1.4%) and South Asia's
is comparably small (2 genomes, 1.3%). This unexplained-resistance gap is
a genuine, previously undocumented limitation of the 8-feature model,
specific to the Norway population, and likely contributes to Norway's
residual sensitivity of 0.881 (lowest of the three populations).
