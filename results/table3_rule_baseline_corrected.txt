MAJOR FINDING: original paper's rule-based AUROC=0.500 in Norway and
South Asia was an artifact of the gyrA bug (near-universal false-positive
firing), not a genuine baseline failure. Corrected rule performs nearly
as well as the elastic-net model in Norway (0.945 vs 0.968) but still
fails substantially in South Asia (0.731 vs 0.970). This changes the
paper's core comparator narrative: the naive rule is not uniformly
useless externally -- its failure is specific to populations with
different resistance-mechanism composition, not to geography per se.
